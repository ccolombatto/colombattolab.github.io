DR. CLARA COLOMBATTO, Principal Investigator [CV]
 
Dr. Colombatto is an Assistant Professor at the University of Waterloo, in the School of Psychology, and an Honorary Lecturer at University College London. Prior to joining UW, Dr. Colombatto was a Research fellow at UCL. She earned a PhD in Psychology at Yale University, and a BS in Neuroscience and Philosophy from Duke University. Dr. Colombatto's research uses methods from vision science, cognitive psychology, and social psychology to investigate how we perceive other agents and their mental states.
DR. TRENT CASH, Postdoctoral Fellow
 
Dr. Cash is Postdoctoral Fellow in the School of Psychology at the University of Waterloo. He earned a PhD in Psychology and Social & Decision Sciences from Carnegie Mellon University and a BA in Psychology and Public Management, Leadership, and Policy from The Ohio State University. Dr. Cash's research focuses on the role that metacognition plays in complex reasoning tasks - such as judgment and decision making - for both humans and artificially intelligent agents.

CARTER SMITH, MA Student
 
Carter is a second-year master’s student investigating how the physical factors that underlie our movements, like velocity, shape other people’s inferences about our mental states. He also explores how these inferences bias human attention. Outside the lab, Carter enjoys cycling, running, reading philosophy, and watching baseball.

ANDY BANG, Honours Student
 
Andy is in his 4th year of Psychology (BSc) at the University of Waterloo.
